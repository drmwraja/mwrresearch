Innovating Materials for a Sustainable Energy Future

MWR Research Group at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI) is at the forefront of next-generation energy storage research, developing advanced energy materials, monolithic all-solid-state batteries (m-ASSBs), high-performance ceramic solid electrolytes, sustainable paper-based separators for lithium-ion and sodium-ion batteries and supercapacitors, interface-engineered functional materials, and advanced electrochemical energy storage technologies for a sustainable future.
