ABOUT THE RESEARCHER

Science-driven development of next-generation energy technologies

Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata, working in the Energy Materials & Devices Division.

His research integrates ceramic science, electrochemistry, nanotechnology and interface engineering to develop advanced materials and architectures for energy-storage applications.

Current research interests include all-solid-state batteries, fast lithium-ion conducting ceramic electrolytes, interface engineering, paper-based separators, redox-flow battery membranes and sustainable carbon materials.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

Materials, interfaces and architectures for advanced electrochemical energy systems.

01

Solid-State Batteries

Development of all-solid-state battery architectures, ceramic electrolytes and cathode–electrolyte interfaces.

02

LLZO Ceramic Electrolytes

Garnet-type lithium-ion conducting ceramics, densification, grain-boundary engineering and sintering strategies.

03

Interface Engineering

Engineering solid–solid and solid–liquid interfaces to reduce impedance and improve electrochemical stability.

04

Paper-Based Battery Materials

PAPERATOR and PAPELYTE platforms for thin, porous, flexible and thermally stable battery components.

05

Redox Flow Batteries

Cellulosic and composite ion-exchange membranes for vanadium redox-flow battery systems.

06

Sustainable Carbon Materials

Waste-derived activated and turbostratic carbon materials for electrochemical energy-storage applications.

TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT

From Materials to Technology

Selected research platforms and technology development activities.

01

PAPERATOR

Paper-based ceramic separator technology combining a cellulosic framework with ceramic functionality for advanced lithium-ion battery applications.

02

PAPELYTE

Paper-based solid electrolyte/separator platform designed to provide flexibility, porosity, wettability and ceramic electrochemical functionality.

03

Ga-LLZO

Engineered garnet electrolyte platform using microstructure, sintering and interface engineering for improved lithium-ion transport.

04

Monolithic SSB

Cathode–glass–electrolyte monolithic architecture for scalable all-solid-state battery development.

RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

Engineering materials, interfaces and architectures for practical energy technologies.

The research approach connects fundamental materials science with device-level engineering, scalability and technology translation.
