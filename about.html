Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute, Kolkata, working in the field of advanced electrochemical energy storage materials. His research integrates ceramic science, electrochemistry, nanotechnology and interface engineering to develop next-generation lithium-ion batteries, all-solid-state batteries, sodium-ion batteries, supercapacitors and sustainable energy materials. He has contributed significantly to ceramic solid electrolytes, paper-based battery separators, ion conducting membranes and commercially scalable battery technologies.
