Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist in the Energy Materials & Devices Division at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata. His research focuses on advanced electrochemical energy storage materials, including all-solid-state batteries, ceramic solid electrolytes, lithium-ion batteries, sodium-ion batteries, supercapacitors, sustainable paper-based separators, and interface engineering. His research bridges fundamental materials science with scalable technologies for next-generation clean energy solutions.
