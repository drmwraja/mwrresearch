PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Principal Scientist in Energy Materials & Devices

Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata, India.

His scientific work focuses on the development of advanced materials and architectures for electrochemical energy storage, with particular emphasis on solid-state batteries and lithium-ion conducting ceramic electrolytes.

His research combines materials chemistry, ceramic processing, electrochemistry, nanotechnology and interface engineering to address challenges associated with next-generation energy-storage systems.

Research activities also include paper-based battery components, redox-flow battery membranes and sustainable carbon materials derived from renewable and waste resources.
