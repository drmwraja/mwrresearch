About the Principal Investigator

Driving innovation in advanced energy materials and electrochemical energy storage technologies.

Dr. Mir Wasim Raja

Principal Scientist

Energy Materials & Devices Division
CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI)
Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR)
Kolkata, India

Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist in the Energy Materials & Devices Division at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata. His research focuses on all-solid-state batteries, ceramic solid electrolytes, lithium-ion batteries, sodium-ion batteries, supercapacitors, sustainable paper-based separators, and interface engineering for advanced electrochemical energy storage systems.

His work integrates advanced ceramic materials, scalable manufacturing strategies, and electrochemical characterization to develop safer, high-performance, and sustainable energy storage technologies for future electric mobility and renewable energy applications.
